The Exposure Triangle

Every photo is made of three controls. Master them one at a time, then combine them.

🔆

ISO

Sensor sensitivity to light. Higher ISO brightens a dark scene, but adds grainy noise.

⭕

Aperture (f-stop)

The size of the lens opening. A low f-number = wide opening = more light + blurry background (shallow depth of field). A high f-number = everything sharp, less light.

⏱️

Shutter Speed

How long the sensor sees light. Fast shutter freezes motion. Slow shutter blurs motion and gathers more light.

Watch the light meter — center it for a correct exposure. Then hit Capture to check your shot against the brief.
